HOW TO READ THE EXPERIMENT

Each NMF lane shows when one recurring frequency pattern becomes active. These are spectral components, not verified instrument stems. One instrument may create several components, and two instruments can share a component. Click a gold onset to select a sound; the cyan match lane shows other events with similar attack spectra. Click any event again to jump playback to that moment.

If the candidate attacks align with bass notes but NMF cannot distinguish bass and melody, we'll know to focus on timbre grouping or guided separation. If the attacks are missing too, the event detector needs improvement before we use its output to drive visuals.
